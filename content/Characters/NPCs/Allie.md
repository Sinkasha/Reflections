# Allie

Engaged to [[Sarah]]. She was killed by some creature sent by [[God of Death Nyarlathotep|Nyarlathotep]], an aberration, possibly a woestrider, who showed up and killed her. Her mother is [[Violet]].

According to [[Gabriel]] she may be alive but the authenticity of the fact is questionable. He said that Allie is rebelling against the "preordained notion of fate". She angered the woestrider, causing it to swallow her whole and is currently in a catatonic state, alive but preserved. However, in the battle against Tabitha and Gabriel, after destroying the orb, Sarah sees a vision of the woestrider and Allie, and somehow gets the feeling that she is alive and can be saved.
