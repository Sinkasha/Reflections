# Derrick Fiskov

The Formerly Cursed Shopkeeper

Currently traveling with the party after they cure him of his curse.

His cousin is [[Melina Fiskov]]. He has a sister, [[Fae Fiskov]], who was originally entrusted with the [[Silver Key with Fusillan Flag|silver key]].

Derrick and his sister Fae used to live together in [[Parli]], working as blacksmiths. Derrick ran the shop. However, after the Fusillan government stopped wanting to ship weapons, Derrick left and moved to [[Ashfall]] while Fae stayed behind.
