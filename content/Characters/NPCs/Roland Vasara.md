# Roland Vasara

The founder of the [[Vasara Adventuring Company]] and older brother to Sorean (now [[Bloo]]).  It was Sorean's dream to found the VAC so Roland founded it in his name. Roland never stopped looking for Sorean, believing him to be alive.
