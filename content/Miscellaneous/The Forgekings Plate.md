# The Forgekings Plate

Crafted by [[Wilfred Dongle]]
- AC: 20
- If the wearer does not have at least 15 strength, their speed is reduced by 15 feet.
- **Peak Craftsmanship**: You gain resistance to fire damage and all incoming damage is reduced by 2.
- **Infernal Vengeance**: When you succeed on a saving throw, you may use your reaction to cast Hellish Rebuke at 1st level (2d10 fire). This can be used twice per long rest.
